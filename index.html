Who to meet

Picked from the 2026 exhibitor list, grouped by why a conversation with Maison Tricot could lead somewhere. Categories come from the company names and what I know of these brands, so check each on the floor.

Reconnect

A relationship you already have.

Tribù

Booth 2667

Belgian outdoor furniture. Named as a long-standing collaborator on your own website. Ask how they plan to show textile in the US.

Belgian company in the hall

Easy introductions, and they know the same supply chain.

Brands that could use your textile

Furniture and outdoor brands where a custom or developed textile adds a collection. Your pitch to "interior brands" lands here.

Lighting with fabric in it

Shades, diffusers and screens. A natural fit for the lighting applications on your site.

Textile and wall peers

Study their stands, their mills and how they present contract performance (fire rating, cleanability). They also sell to the same designers.

Showrooms and culture

Places worth an hour on the Tuesday, or any free evening. I found no announced November launch parties at these showrooms, so book a visit directly.

Kvadrat

475 Park Ave at 58th St

Two showrooms side by side: contract (design by Jonathan Olivares) and residential (Vincent Van Duysen). The closest peer set to what you make. Kvadrat also works with Raf Simons, a name on your own client list.

Arte flagship

979 Third Ave, 9th floor, D&D Building

Arte's wallcovering showroom and atelier, opened 2025. Arte also exhibits at BDNY (booth 2737, shared with Koroseal).

200 Lex, New York Design Center

200 Lexington Ave

A building of trade showrooms. Open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5:30 pm, some Saturdays. Textile, lighting and furniture houses in one place.

SoHo showrooms

Wooster, Greene, Grand St

Baxter, Flos, Lemieux et Cie and Scavolini, from the Aperitivo route. Walk it any day.

Cooper Hewitt

Upper East Side

"Design Across Time" fills the first floor and includes textiles and wallcoverings. Close to COLLECTIBLE on East 86th.

The Museum at FIT

W 27th St and 7th Ave

"Doll Dressing" runs to 3 January 2027. Same campus as the Friday Future Tense talk.

To confirm before you go

Open points
  • COLLECTIBLE VIP preview: the fair's site says Thursday 5 November, the NYCxDesign page says Thursday 4 November.
  • Aperitivo in SoHo has no date on its listing. Ask SoHo Design District.
  • BDNY opening hours are not on the pages I could read. One exhibitor lists 10 am to 5 pm on both days.
  • BDNY's "Booth Pop-ups + Happenings" schedule had not been published yet, and the page still shows 2025 dates. Check again closer to the fair.
  • One trade-show aggregator lists a hotel show, "HX: The Hotel Experience", on 10–11 November at Javits. I could not confirm it on an official page, so I left it out.
  • I found no brand-specific launch events for the exhibitors above. Ask your contacts at Tribù, Kvadrat and Mokum what they have planned.
